ALPHI

Agnostic latent phenotype identification: groups patients described by Human Phenotype Ontology terms, without diagnostic labels. Everything runs in this browser tab; the patient data are not sent anywhere.

1. Patients

JSON in the PytheasDB / ALPHI format (a list of patients with id, gene and symptoms), or a CSV/TSV table with columns id, hpo (HPO codes separated by ;), and optionally gene, status (alive/dead) and age (years, at death or last news).
